Privacy Policy

HoloHA Mail and Packages is a personal, single-user application. The only user is its owner, who authorizes access to their own Gmail account.

What the app accesses

The app connects to the owner's Gmail mailbox over IMAP using OAuth 2.0 and reads email subject lines and USPS Informed Delivery images in order to count packages and mail pieces expected on the current day.

How data is handled

  • All processing happens locally on the owner's own Home Assistant server.
  • No email content or account data is sent to, stored by, or shared with any third party or external service.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or used to train any model.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only within the owner's Home Assistant instance and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

Contact

Questions can be raised by opening an issue on this repository.
